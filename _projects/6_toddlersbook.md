This is a project I had of translating two book authored by [Prof. Elizabeth A. Quaglia](https://lizquaglia.wordpress.com/), Learning Together - Cryptography for Toddlers and Learning Together - Cybersecurity for Toddlers.

These books were written to sparkle  :sparkles: curiosity of cryptography and cybersecurity in children, with the help of an adult to guide to reading.
