## When sudo-apt update doesn't work

My first issue with the discipline was way before anything could work out. 

```sh
GPG error: file:/var/cuda-repo-10-2-local-10.2.89-440.33.01 Release: The following signatures couldn't be verified because the public key is not available: NO_PUBKEY F60F4B3D7FA2AF80
```

This warning haunted me for days, when doing `sudo apt-update` until I could actually install anything. Basically, the GPG key manager wouldn't allow me to `sudo apt-update` and yays, no solutions could be done. 

Google crawling was the only solution:

## First trial:

My first option was trying to follow [this](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/updating-the-cuda-linux-gpg-repository-key/)tutorial on the NVIDIA developers blog.

Of course it didn't work out, and I just felt like randomly copying and pasting random key deletion and insertion.


## Second trial:

After this pointless tutorial, I just tried to search for [other](https://blog.csdn.net/sunyuhua_keyboard/article/details/136215517) and [other](https://blog.csdn.net/bearrex/article/details/125060918), and [other](https://forums.developer.nvidia.com/t/updating-the-cuda-linux-gpg-repository-key/212897/22)...

And none of them worked out.

As if it couldn't get better, even the NVIDIA moderators seemed to not know exactly where and why the PubKey of older version kept having issues even if CUDA handled deprecated versions.

## Thrid trial:

Finally, trying to search for the specific key in many forums, I found a solution [here](https://askubuntu.com/questions/831386/gpgkeys-key-f60f4b3d7fa2af80-not-found-on-keyserver)

Basically, I ran thin:

```sh 
wget -qO - https://developer.download.nvidia.com/compute/cuda/repos/ubuntu1804/x86_64/7fa2af80.pub | sudo apt-key add -

```

And magically, `sudo-apt update` worked out
